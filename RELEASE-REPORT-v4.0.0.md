## Deliberate SEO decisions
- Keep Service + Offer for rental/task pages instead of forcing Product markup.
- Do not add self-serving AggregateRating/Review stars to LocalBusiness.
- Do not publish customer names without explicit permission.
- LocalBusiness no longer claims the variable pickup point as a permanent street/geo location.
- FAQPage remains valid semantic markup, although Google generally does not show FAQ rich results for ordinary commercial sites.
